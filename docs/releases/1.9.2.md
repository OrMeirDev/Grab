גרסת התוסף: **1.3.2**. הורדה רגילה, PDF, העיצוב וכלי ההורדה נשמרו.

Fix small HLS/DASH manifests being discarded by the tiny-media size heuristic. Manifest size
describes the playlist rather than the video; retain the existing filtering of short files, ads,
initialization tracks and segments. Update Grab, Repair integration and Reload extension 1.3.2.
